**Out of Melee** shows a small red **!** when your target is out of melee range. No bars, no numbers, just the "!", wherever you want it.

It checks the range of one of your melee abilities. That check isn't hidden from addons, so it keeps working in combat, in WoW Forever too.

### Classes

- **Warrior, Rogue, Death Knight, Monk, Demon Hunter**
- **Paladin:** Holy Strike in WoW Forever (from level 6), Crusader Strike or Rebuke elsewhere
- **Druid:** only in Cat and Bear Form
- **Shaman:** needs Stormstrike (or Primal Strike / Lava Lash where they exist)
- **Hunter:** only Survival in retail

Casters get nothing, since they don't have a melee ability to check.

### Settings

You find them in the game options under AddOns → Out of Melee.

- **Show Anchor** lets you drag the "!" where you want it
- **Only in combat**
- Size and color
- Turn it off for a single character, like your healer

Works in Retail, WoW Forever, Classic Era, TBC, Cata and Mists Classic.
