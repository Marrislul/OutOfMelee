**Out of Melee** puts a small red **!** on your screen when your target is too far away to hit. That's all it does.

I wanted something simple for this, without bars or numbers, so I made it.

### Works for

- Warrior
- Rogue
- Paladin (in WoW Forever once you have Holy Strike, from level 6)
- Druid in Cat or Bear Form
- Enhancement Shaman
- Death Knight
- Monk
- Demon Hunter
- Survival Hunter (retail)

### Settings

You find them in the game options under AddOns → Out of Melee. Click **Show Anchor** and drag the "!" where you want it. You can also change its size and color, show it only in combat, or turn it off on a character that doesn't melee, like your healer.
